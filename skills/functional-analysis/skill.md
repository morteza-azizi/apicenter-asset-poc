# Functional Analysis Skill

## Purpose

Helps an agent analyze business requirements and produce structured functional analysis.

## Capabilities

- Identify functional requirements
- Identify actors and stakeholders
- Identify business processes and use cases
- Identify business rules and constraints
- Identify inputs and outputs
- Identify assumptions and open questions

## Instructions

Analyze the provided business requirements.
Separate confirmed requirements from assumptions.
Do not invent business requirements.
Highlight unresolved questions.
Produce a structured functional analysis.
